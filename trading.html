Trading Stores & Supermarkets Sector

Community-Owned Retail Supermarkets, Wholesale Supply Chains & Steady Shareholder Dividends

Generating Sustainable Income Through Community Commerce

Managed under the General Manager Trading Stores & Supermarkets Section, this commercial wing operates retail outlets and supply chains that provide affordable household goods to Ntungamo residents while generating steady revenue for shareholders and the church treasury.

By pooling member capital and partnering with local suppliers and agents, our trading stores keep profits circulating inside the community under strict financial accountability.

Retail & Wholesale Operations

Hover over a line to view details:

  • Community Supermarkets ▾
    Well-stocked retail outlets offering everyday groceries, household essentials, and school supplies at fair community prices.
  • Wholesale & Bulk Distribution ▾
    Bulk sourcing and supply partnerships supporting local shopkeepers, schools, and member businesses across Ntungamo.
  • Member Produce & Supplier Market ▾
    Creating reliable retail shelf space and market access for goods produced by SACCO members and local farmers.

Investor & Member Benefits
