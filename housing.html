Land & Housing Finance Sector

Structured Property Acquisition • Affordable Housing • House Improvement Financing in Ntungamo

Building Generational Wealth Through Land & Homes

Managed under the General Manager Land & Housing, this sector empowers SACCO members, church congregations, and community partners to acquire verified plots, construct residential homes, and upgrade existing properties across Ntungamo Municipality and surrounding areas.

Through flexible installment plans, group land-buying schemes, and dedicated House Improvement Loans, members turn steady savings into lasting real estate assets.

Housing & Land Programs

Hover over a program to view details:

  • House Improvement Loan ▾
    Financing for roofing, plastering, fencing, solar installation, and home renovations with flexible repayment terms.
  • Plot & Land Acquisition ▾
    Structured credit and group land-purchase schemes for verified residential and commercial plots in Ntungamo.
  • Phased Construction Support ▾
    Step-by-step building material and construction financing aligned with your SACCO savings growth.

Requirements & Verification

Hover over a requirement to view details:

  • Active SACCO Savings Account ▾
    Maintain an active Personal, Daily, or Group Saving Account to qualify for property and housing facilities.
  • Ownership & Sale Documentation ▾
    Valid land title, customary sale agreement, or LC1 & family consent verification for property security.
  • Project Budget & Guarantors ▾
    Clear bill of quantities or construction plan backed by approved SACCO member guarantors.
