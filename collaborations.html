Business Collaborations & Strategic Partnerships

Connecting Investors, Agents, Digital Innovators & Community Enterprises Across Ntungamo & Beyond

Growing Together Through Strategic Alliances

Guided by our motto "Together We Achieve", the Business Collaborations wing opens doors for individual investors, diaspora supporters, field agents, and partner institutions to co-build sustainable enterprises with Ntungamo Municipality Born Again Community sacco .

Every partnership is governed by clear commitment agreements, audited financial reporting, and our core values of Accountability and Transparency.
