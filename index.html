A Decade of Proven Stewardship Since 2015

Founded in 2001 in Ntungamo, Uganda by Bp. Stempson Kamwesigye and Pastor Oliva Nalubega Kamwesigye at Revival Centre Cathedral Church. In October 2015, Church Administrator Mr. Kizito Cosmas, alongside Mr. Mukole Johnbosco and Miss Arinaitwe Peress, researched sustainable projects to support church operations and empower believers.

By the end of 2015, local church members raised an initial share capital of UGX 830,000 to launch a faith-grounded financial institution, officially registered with the district local government as Ntungamo Municipality Born Again Community sacco (BADG).
