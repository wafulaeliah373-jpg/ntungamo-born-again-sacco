Academic & Computer Skilling Sector

Empowering Youth & Members with Practical ICT Skills, Education Savings & Faith-Based Learning

Equipping the Community Through Education & Digital Skills

The Academic Sector invests in quality faith-based education, student savings plans, and hands-on vocational training across Ntungamo Municipality.

In partnership with Abacos ICT Centre, we run practical Computer Skilling short courses that help members, students, and entrepreneurs explore modern business skills and digital tools.

  • Junior Saving Account (Under 18) ▾
    Dedicated school-fees savings plan opened with UGX 10,000/= and zero monthly charges to secure children's education.
  • Institutional & School Support ▾
    Financial partnerships and scholarships supporting Christian schools and learners within Ntungamo Municipality.
