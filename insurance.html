The Family Protection Plan

Under the Insurance Sector, Ntungamo Municipality Born Again Community sacco provides accessible, community-centered welfare and risk protection tailored for SACCO members, church congregations, and families across Ntungamo Municipality.

By pooling small, affordable subscriptions together, members ensure rapid financial relief during medical emergencies, bereavement, and household crises without depleting their personal savings.

Protection Coverage

Hover over a benefit to view details:

  • Bereavement & Funeral Support ▾
    Immediate financial assistance and logistical support for the nuclear family and registered dependents.
  • Emergency Medical Relief ▾
    Hospitalization cash support to help cover urgent inpatient medical bills for covered family members.
  • Loan Protection Cover ▾
    Protects a borrower's family and guarantors from debt burden in the event of permanent disability or loss of life.

How to Subscribe

Hover over a requirement to view details:

  • Active SACCO Membership ▾
    Open to all registered members and shareholders of Ntungamo Municipality Born Again Community SACCO.
  • Dependent Registration ▾
    Submit National ID copies or birth details for your spouse, children, and approved next of kin.
  • Regular Welfare Subscription ▾
    Maintain active contributions through your Personal or Daily Saving Account with zero hidden penalties.

THE FUNERAL INSURANCE FUND PROJECT (F.I.F.)

Terms and Conditions That Apply

  1. Annual Subscription: The BADO Funeral Insurance Fund Programme (F.I.F) runs on an annual basis (1 full year); renewal is optional.
  2. Subscription Fee: 70,000 UGX (Fifty/Seventy Thousand Shillings, subject to update due to prevailing circumstances).
  3. Grace Period: New or starting subscribers undergo a four (4) months maturing period after payment before benefits become active.
  4. Benefit Requirements & Payout:
    • Beneficiaries MUST be registered by exact name on this form (vacant spaces declare no beneficiary; updates allowed only for newly biologically born children and spouse).
    • Subscriber or Next of Kin MUST immediately inform the office upon death before burial ceremonies; the Organisation sends its representative to witness the burial.
    • Where multiple subscribers share a common deceased beneficiary, a single Fund is considered toward that beneficiary.
    • Fund Amount: Currently, the Organisation offers500,000 UGX (Five hundred thousand shillings) upon loss of a registered beneficiary.
